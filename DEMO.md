# Two-minute TransitGap demonstration

Use the deployed site and the prepared real VTA example. Do not submit fabricated public reports.

1. **0:00–0:20:** Open `/`. Explain the last part of a transit journey: distance alone does not reveal stairs, crossings or missing records. Scroll the actual network preview and open the map.
2. **0:20–0:45:** Choose “Try a real Diridon journey”, leave the wheelchair profile selected, and find the route. Read the distance in miles. Open directions and inspect an unknown or concern segment. Mapped information is not field verification.
3. **0:45–1:05:** Compare with the standard route. Show the measured mobility detour and explain the different speed assumptions. If the routes match, say so; do not invent a dramatic detour.
4. **1:05–1:30:** Open Access gaps. Select a stop from the evaluated sample and show both ten-minute networks. Explain that the ranking covers 32 sampled stops, and that speeds, constraints and missing data affect the result.
5. **1:30–1:45:** Prepare a letter, edit the draft and download it. Nothing has been sent. Open Evidence to distinguish City records, OSM tags and unverified observations. Public reporting is paused pending durable storage.
6. **1:45–2:00:** Open Under the hood and compare the five algorithms. Show the public MCP endpoint `https://transitgap.vercel.app/mcp`, or run `TRANSITGAP_MCP_URL=https://transitgap.vercel.app/mcp backend/.venv/bin/python scripts/smoke_mcp.py`. Finish with the actual limits and next field-validation step.

Schedule entries must be described as **scheduled, not real-time**. If the feed is not effective on the recording date, show its dates and unavailable departures honestly. The absence of a scheduled departure is not a route-planning failure.
